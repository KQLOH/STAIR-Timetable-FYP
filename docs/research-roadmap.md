# Immediate research-preparation roadmap

All deliverables below are proposals unless actual approval/evidence is supplied. Application integration and synthetic software tests do not perform this research work or formal evaluation.

1. **Agree on work ownership and reference access.** Identify the approved Research Report, Supervisor / Moderator Review and Supervision Answers through authorized private access. Preserve RO1–RO4/RQ1–RQ4; do not invent missing definitions.
2. **Source inventory preparation.** Student B improves the [blank inventory draft](../templates/source-inventory-template.csv) and records questions about version, source lineage, dependencies, permission evidence and timezone authority. The draft is not the approved M2 schema. Actual source/permission/identity records stay private; unknown values remain unknown.
3. **Annotation-guide proposal.** Use the [guide outline](../templates/annotation-guide-proposal.md) to gather supervisor decisions, required/conditional fields, ambiguity and source-first/blind workflow requirements. Do not create actual annotations or call a proposal approved.
4. **29-page audit workflow preparation.** Use the [audit outline](../templates/audit-29-page-workflow-proposal.md) to plan frame eligibility, approved sampling, actual independent roles, blind comparison/adjudication and retained evidence. Do not select 29 pages, open held-out labels or perform the audit in this public repository.
5. **Approved synthetic planning and review.** Both students can propose invented examples, test expectations and documentation checks without private source content. Public examples must be clearly synthetic; they do not prove Gold correctness or measured system accuracy.

Acceptance for this preparation stage is a reviewed proposal plus a factual list of missing decisions, not Gold creation, audit completion, PRE closure, freeze or evaluation. Student A coordinates publication responsibilities and scoped system development; Student B coordinates preparation documentation. Actual human research activities require their own prerequisites/approval and independent reviewers.
